Every workspace has its own plan, shared by its Discord server and Telegram group. Plans differ in monthly [credits](/billing/credits), capacity limits, and a few advanced features. Answering, auto-learning, the calendar, and basic analytics work on every plan.

| | **Free** | **Pro** | **Team** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Price | \$0 | \$20 / month | \$50 / month |
| Credits per month | 100 | 500 | 1,200 |
| Knowledge sources | 10 | 50 | Unlimited |
| Monitored channels or topics | 3 | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| History import | Last month | Last 6 months | Last 6 months |
| Team members | 3 | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Auto-learning, calendar, basic analytics | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| AI analytics: community mood, topics, suggestions | — | ✓ | ✓ |
| On-demand AI reports | — | ✓ | ✓ |
| Announcements as bot knowledge | — | ✓ | ✓ |
| Learning from closed tickets | — | ✓ | ✓ |
| Weekly AI report by email | — | — | ✓ |
| White-label bot | — | — | ✓ |

## How to get Pro or Team

- **Buy a plan** — on the **Billing** page, click **Manage** and pick Pro or Team. Payment is made in crypto and covers one 30-day period. Renew from the same page before it ends.
- **Pro trial** — every workspace is offered a one-time 14-day Pro trial, once per Discord server or Telegram group. See [Promo codes & trial](/billing/promo-codes).
- **Promo codes** — a code grants a workspace a plan for a fixed number of days.

Your current plan, its remaining days, and your credit balance are always visible on the **Billing** page.

## What happens when a paid plan ends

The workspace falls back to Free. Nothing is deleted — but if you're over the Free limits (e.g. more than 10 knowledge sources), you won't be able to add new ones until you're back under the limit or on a higher plan.
