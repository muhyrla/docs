<Accordion title="The bot isn't answering — what do I check?">
In order of likelihood:

1. **Is the bot enabled?** Check the master switch in [Bot settings](/bot/settings).
2. **Is the channel selected?** The bot only answers in channels (or Telegram topics) you've picked in Bot settings.
3. **Do you have credits?** At zero balance the bot pauses — see the [Billing](/billing/credits) page.
4. **Is your knowledge Ready?** Sources still in Pending/Processing/Error aren't used in answers.
5. **Does your knowledge actually cover the question?** If not, the bot deliberately stays silent instead of guessing — see [How the bot answers](/bot/how-answers-work).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A knowledge source is stuck in Error. What now?">
Open the source — the error message says what went wrong. Common causes: an unreachable or login-protected URL, or an unsupported file format. Fix the cause and re-add or reindex the source. Failed ingestion doesn't leave broken knowledge behind.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What file types can I upload?">
Only `.md` (Markdown) and `.txt`. Export other formats to plain text first. See [Uploading files](/knowledge/files).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can the bot make things up?">
No. It answers strictly from your knowledge base and links its sources in every reply. If it has nothing relevant, it stays silent and routes the question to your trusted members via [auto-learning](/knowledge/auto-learning).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Do members need to mention the bot to get an answer?">
No — the bot detects questions in monitored channels automatically. Members just ask naturally.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I get more credits?">
Credits refill each billing cycle from your plan's allowance. To get more: claim the one-time [Pro trial](/billing/promo-codes), redeem a promo code, or buy a higher [plan](/billing/plans) on the **Billing** page.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I connect several Discord servers?">
Yes. Each server becomes its own workspace with an isolated knowledge base, team, plan, and credit balance. Switch between them in the dashboard sidebar.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Does Relayn work in Telegram?">
Yes. Connect a Telegram group with a one-time code and `/connect` — see [Connecting Telegram](/telegram). A workspace can have one Discord server and one Telegram group; they share the knowledge base, learned answers, and credits.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="The bot doesn't answer in my Telegram group">
  1. **Is the bot still in the group and an administrator?** The Telegram card in Bot settings shows whether Relayn can read every message.
  2. **Does the group use topics?** Then the bot only answers in the topics you selected. A new topic appears in the dashboard after someone writes in it.
  3. **Is the person asking an expert?** The bot doesn't answer group administrators or experts — it learns from them instead.
  4. **Is the bot paused?** Check the **Bot is active** switch on the Telegram card.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="/connect says the code is wrong or expired">
  Codes work for 15 minutes and only once, and requesting a new code cancels the previous one. Get a fresh code in Bot settings and make sure a group administrator sends it.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Do Tickets work in Telegram?">
  Not yet. Telegram has no ticket channels, so Tickets work with Discord servers only for now. Everything else — answers, auto-learning, the calendar, analytics — works on both platforms.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Who can teach the bot new answers?">
  In Discord, only members you've marked as trusted (by role or individually) in [Bot settings](/bot/settings). In Telegram, group administrators and the [experts](/telegram#experts-who-the-bot-learns-from) you added. Answers from anyone else are never learned.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I removed a knowledge source — will the bot still use it?">
No. Deleted sources drop out of answers immediately.
</Accordion>
